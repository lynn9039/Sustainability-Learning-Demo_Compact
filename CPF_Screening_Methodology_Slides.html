Introduction

What Is Climate Pledge Friendly?

Launched in 2020, CPF helps Amazon customers discover and shop for products with sustainability features. It is not itself a certification — it recognizes third-party certifications relevant to climate, environment, human health, and social welfare.

Methodology Overview

Two-Tier Screening Structure

The methodology operates at two levels to ensure both the certification program and the individual product meet CPF standards.

Tier 1

Certification-Level

Evaluates the strength of certification protocols: governance, credibility, verification methods, and scope of sustainability claims.

Tier 2

Product-Level

Certification-Level Requirements

Three Core Screening Themes

Theme 01

Foundational Programmatic

Must address climate or environmental impacts. Must recognize product-level sustainability improvements. Must establish fixed, absolute performance thresholds with independent verification.

Theme 02

Credibility, Data & Governance

12 requirements covering: regular standard updates, assessor independence, verification methods, public disclosure, mandatory language, and recognized accreditation (EPA, ISEAL, ISO 14024, etc.).

Theme 03

Scope & Relevance

Determines if a certification sufficiently addresses the sustainability issues it claims. Assessed per product category for granular accuracy. Three pathways: Carbon, Energy, Multi-Attribute.

Scope & Relevance

Three Certification Scopes

🌡️

Carbon-Focused

Certifications verifying carbon reduction or carbon reducing + offsets. Requires GHG Protocol compliance, third-party assurance, reduction plans, and full lifecycle measurement (cradle-to-grave). Carbon neutral certifications are being phased out.

⚡

Energy-Focused

Certifications verifying energy reduction at point-of-use (electronics only) or manufacturing phase (all categories). Manufacturing pathway requires chain of custody and covers electricity reduction, on-site renewables, and off-site EACs.

🌿

Multi-Attribute

Certifications addressing multiple sustainability indicators. Scored across 10 attributes per product category using a green/yellow/red framework. Must achieve at least 2 greens, or 1 green + 2 yellows, to pass.

Multi-Attribute Framework

10 Sustainability Attributes

  • Climate Impact — GHG emissions reduction & sequestration
  • Energy Use — Manufacturing & point-of-use energy consumption
  • Soil Health — Regenerative practices, pesticide restrictions
  • Solid Waste — Waste reduction, recycled inputs, refill/reuse
  • Water Use — Water reduction, reuse/recycling in operations
  • Biodiversity — Ecosystem conversion bans, habitat restoration
  • Workers' Health & Welfare — ILO conventions, labor rights
  • Animal Welfare — Five domains: nutrition, environment, health, behavior, mental state
  • Pollution / Chemicals — RSL compliance, wastewater, air quality
  • Ecosystem / Resource Use — Sustainable harvesting, indigenous rights (FPIC)

Scoring: Each attribute is rated Green (1 strong or 2 moderate actions), Yellow (1 moderate), or Red (none). Pass requires ≥2 Greens or 1 Green + 2 Yellows per product category.

Product-Level Requirements

Additional Guardrails for Products

Even after a certification passes, individual products must meet additional due diligence requirements before receiving the CPF badge.

Material Thresholds

≥ 50%

Certified material by weight for material-claim certifications (e.g., FSC wood, organic cotton)

Recycled Content

10–50%

Varies by product category. 50% for textiles, 30% for smartphones, 10% for gaming devices & TVs

Exclusion List

11 Categories

Beef, gas-powered tools, aerosols, single-use plastic bottles, fur, e-cigarettes, glitter, and more

Reduction thresholds: Energy and water reduction claims require ≥10% verified reduction against a specified baseline, varying by product category.

Governance

Change Model & Reassessment

Guiding Principles

  • Prioritize requirements that meaningfully improve product sustainability
  • Rely on established sustainability science and expert validation
  • Create clear, repeatable evaluation requirements for consistent decisions
  • Recognize and amplify industry-leading sustainability innovation

Review Cadence

  • Screening requirements reviewed and updated regularly for scientific rigor
  • Biennial reassessment of all existing certifications
  • Updated criteria take immediate effect for new applications
  • Existing certifications reassessed at next biennial review
  • 30-day appeal window for certifications that do not pass

Critical Review: The methodology is reviewed by 5 external stakeholders who evaluate the methodology, reasoning, and assumptions.
